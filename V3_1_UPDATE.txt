TAGALOG TRAINER v3.1 — SLOW BUTTON FIX

WHAT WAS WRONG
The previous Slow button used 0.65x. On iPhone voices that can sound very close
to the normal 0.85x setting. The natural voice also needed a stronger learner-
slow instruction.

WHAT CHANGED
- Slow button is now a fixed 0.45x on the device voice.
- Natural voice gets a dedicated learnerSlow mode.
- Natural slow audio is generated at 0.50 speed plus explicit slow-learning
  pronunciation instructions.
- Slow and normal natural audio use separate cache entries.
- The app shows a "Slow learner pronunciation" message when the button is tapped.
- Service-worker cache bumped so the update is fetched.

IF YOU USE ONLY THE IPHONE VOICE
Replace these two files in GitHub:
  index.html
  sw.js

IF YOU USE THE NATURAL AI VOICE
Replace these three files:
  index.html
  sw.js
  cloudflare-worker.js

After updating the Cloudflare Worker, redeploy it.

ON IPHONE
1. Commit the replacement files to GitHub.
2. Wait for GitHub Pages to redeploy.
3. Close Tagalog Trainer completely.
4. Open it once in Safari while online, then reopen the Home Screen app.
5. If an old cached version remains, remove the Home Screen icon and add the
   hosted site to Home Screen again.

TEST
Open any card and compare:
  Speak        = normal pronunciation
  Slow 0.45x   = clearly slower learner pronunciation
