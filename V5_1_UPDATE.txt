After GitHub Pages redeploys:
1. Open the hosted GitHub Pages app in Safari.
2. More > Realistic Filipino voice.
3. Voice service URL:
   https://tagalogvoice.mrhudsonmj.workers.dev/speak

   the exact APP_TOKEN stored in Cloudflare.
5. Tap "Check connection".
6. If it says Worker connected to ElevenLabs, tap "Load my voices".
